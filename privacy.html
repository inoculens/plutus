Privacy Policy

INOCULENS PLUTUS — effective 13 October 2026.

The short version

INOCULENS PLUTUS is a local-first business accounting and portfolio tracker. There is no INOCULENS PLUTUS account and no business or portfolio server: your business and portfolio live only in your browser's local storage. Market prices come from public price services. If you enable sync, an encrypted copy is stored on a cloud drive you choose — and only you hold the passphrase that can decrypt it. Optional anonymous analytics (Google Analytics 4) loads only if you press Accept on the cookie banner — Decline or ignoring it means no analytics request is ever made.

Who we are

Data controller: INOCULENS PLUTUS. Contact: create@inoculens.com.

Data we store about you: almost none

We operate no portfolio database and receive no portfolio data from the app. Everything below stays on your device unless you actively send it somewhere yourself:

  • Portfolio data — business accounts, ledgers, trades, settings and price overrides, kept in your browser's local storage so the app works offline and privately.
  • Cookie choice — your Accept / Decline decision for analytics, kept in the same browser storage so the banner does not nag you on every visit.
  • Sync configuration — only if you use sync: which provider you chose, filenames, and sign-in tokens. Your sync passphrase stays in the form unless you explicitly tick “Remember passphrase in this browser”, in which case it is kept in the same browser storage — and it is never transmitted anywhere either way.
  • Price cache — recent market prices and currency rates, to limit network requests.
  • Backup files — only where you choose to download or upload them.

Others who process data when you use the app

  • Hosting (Netlify). This page and the app are static files. Like any web host, Netlify sees standard server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps).
  • Price services. To show live values the app queries public market data: CoinGecko (coin + currency), Frankfurter/ECB rates (currency pair + date) and Stooq (stock symbol). These are market-data lookups only — your portfolio is never sent. These services see your IP address like any website you visit; their own privacy policies apply.
  • Fonts. The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts.
  • Sync destinations — only if you enable sync. Google Drive, Dropbox, MEGA object storage or your own WebDAV server receive a single file encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The provider cannot read it: decryption happens only in your browser with your passphrase, which is never transmitted. Google's sign-in helper script loads only at the moment you press Connect.
  • Sign-in providers. When you connect a drive, Google or Dropbox verify your identity for your drive. INOCULENS PLUTUS receives only access tokens, which stay in your browser.
  • Analytics — only if you press Accept (Google Analytics 4). Page views and approximate device data go to Google. Nothing loads when you Decline or ignore the banner. Details in the next section.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4, only with your consent)

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given by pressing Accept in the cookie banner. The banner uses blocking mode: gtag.js (ID G-0JEDV505S7) is not requested at all until you accept.

  • What Google receives: visited pages, approximate location (derived from IP, which Google truncates/anonymizes), device and browser type, referrer. Your business, portfolio, trades and passphrase are never sent.
  • Settings on our side: 2-month event-data retention (the minimum GA offers), Google signals off, ads personalization off, no data sharing with Google for its own purposes.
  • Withdraw anytime: clear cookies and site data for plutus.inoculens.com in your browser settings — the banner will ask again on your next visit and no further analytics will load. Data already sent follows Google's 2-month retention above.
  • Learn more: Google's GA privacy overview and the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Why each drive permission is needed

  • Google Drive (drive.file): access only to files the INOCULENS PLUTUS app itself created — your other Drive files stay invisible to it.
  • Dropbox (app folder): an isolated app folder INOCULENS PLUTUS can read and write; the rest of your Dropbox is untouched.

How sync encryption works

Before anything leaves your browser, your portfolio is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with the key derived from your passphrase via PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 rounds) and a fresh random salt and nonce on every upload. A wrong passphrase or a tampered file fails closed — nothing is decrypted, nothing is overwritten. There is no recovery: if you lose the passphrase, the encrypted copy cannot be opened by anyone, including us.

Your rights (including GDPR)

Legal bases: your consent for optional features such as analytics and sync, and our legitimate interest in serving a secure static site. Because we hold no portfolio data about you server-side:

  • Access / portability: use Download backup and the CSV exports inside the app at any time.
  • Erasure: use Clear all data in Settings (Danger zone), delete any drive copies, and revoke the app's access in your Google/Dropbox account settings. To delete the local cookie choice too, clear site data — the banner will simply ask again.
  • Objection / restriction: stop using sync (Disconnect), clear site data to withdraw analytics consent, or stop using the app; without local data there is nothing to process.
  • To exercise any right against us directly, contact us at the address above — though in practice there is nothing for us to hand over or delete.

Retention

We retain nothing. Data in your browser persists until you clear it; drive copies persist until you delete them; host server logs follow our hosting provider's retention.

Security

All traffic uses TLS. Sync payloads are end-to-end encrypted as described above. Sign-in tokens live in your browser's storage, so the security of your device and browser profile matters — on a shared device, prefer private browsing and do not tick "remember".

Children

INOCULENS PLUTUS is a general finance utility not directed at children under 16.

Changes
